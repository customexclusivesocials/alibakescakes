Questions, answered

How far in advance should I order?

As early as you can, especially for weddings and dessert tables. Include your event date in your inquiry and Ali will confirm availability.

Do you offer wedding cakes?

Yes. Buttercream, semi-naked and fondant wedding cakes, from single tiers to donut towers, with delivery and set-up available.

Do you offer delivery, and where is pickup?

Pickup is in Welland / Vineland. Delivery is available for an additional fee.

Do you offer gluten-free, dairy-free or vegan desserts?

Yes, options are available. Availability varies by product, so mention your dietary requirements in your inquiry.

Can I request a custom design or send inspiration photos?

Yes. Describe your theme and colours in the inquiry form, then email or message your inspiration photos to Ali on Instagram.

How are custom cake prices determined?

By size, servings, design complexity and requested details. Custom cakes start at $115 and classic cakes at $85.

Do you offer dessert tables?

Yes. Mix cake pops, cupcakes, donuts, macarons, shooters, tarts and more, with delivery and set-up available.

What happens after I submit an inquiry?

Ali reviews your request and replies with a quote and next steps, usually within 24 hours. Pricing, details and pickup or delivery are then confirmed.
